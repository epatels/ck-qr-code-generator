# Privacy Policy

**CK QR Code Generator**
Last updated: 5 November 2026

## The short version

CK QR Code Generator works entirely on your computer. It does not connect to the internet, and it does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

## What the app does with your information

**Information you enter.** Anything you type into the app, such as web addresses, text, Wi-Fi names and passwords, contact details, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, event details and UPI payment details, is used only to draw the QR code on your screen. It stays in your computer's memory while the app is open and is **never saved** by the app, never sent anywhere and never logged.

**Files you choose.** The app reads files only when you pick them: a logo image, or a spreadsheet for batch generation. It writes files only where you tell it to: the QR codes you export, and its settings file. Your spreadsheets and logo images are not copied, uploaded or kept.

**Clipboard.** If you use **Paste**, the app reads your clipboard once, at that moment. If you use **Copy image**, it places the finished QR code image on your clipboard. Nothing else is read or kept.

**Settings.** The app saves a small settings file (`qr_generator_config.json`) on your computer with two things only: your theme (light or dark) and the file path of your default logo, if you set one. It contains no QR code content. In the Microsoft Store version it lives in `%APPDATA%\CK QR Code Generator`; when run from source it sits next to the script. Deleting the file resets these settings.

## What the app does not do

- It makes no network connections.
- It has no accounts, sign-in or advertising.
- It includes no analytics, tracking or crash reporting.
- It does not use your camera, microphone or location.
- It does not share information with anyone, because it never sends any.

## QR codes you create

A QR code contains the information you put into it, and anyone who scans or sees it can read that information. For example, a Wi-Fi code lets anyone who scans it join that network, and a contact or event code shows what you entered. Before you share or print a code, check that it contains only what you mean to share. For UPI payment codes, check the UPI ID carefully, because a payment goes to whoever owns that ID. The app does not process payments.

## The Microsoft Store and Windows

If you install the app from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect information about the download and installation (and, depending on your Windows settings, diagnostic data) under [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). The app itself does not send anything to the developer or to Microsoft.

## Feedback on GitHub

If you choose to leave feedback through the project's GitHub page, what you write is public on GitHub and is handled under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The app never submits feedback for you. Please don't include passwords, payment details or other private information.

## Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
